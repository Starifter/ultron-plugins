Square's hosted MCP server: read and manage payments, orders, customers, the item catalog,
inventory and bookings through Square's API.

After installing, run `/mcp login square` (or press *Sign in* on its page) and approve Ultron in the browser. Its tools act on a live seller account: try it with a sandbox account
first, and every call goes through your permission mode.
