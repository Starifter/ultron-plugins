Canva's hosted MCP server: generate designs from a description, edit existing ones, search
your designs and folders, use your brand kits and templates, export to PDF, PNG, PPTX and
more, and comment.

After installing, run `/mcp login canva` (or press *Sign in* on its page) and approve Ultron in the browser.
