PayPal's hosted MCP server: create and send invoices, create and look up orders, manage
products and subscriptions, issue refunds, and read transactions and disputes.

After installing, run `/mcp login paypal` (or press *Sign in* on its page) and approve Ultron in the browser. Its tools move real money in a live account: sign in to a sandbox
account to try it, and every call goes through your permission mode.
